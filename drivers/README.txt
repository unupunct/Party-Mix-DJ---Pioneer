Pioneer DDJ-SB Windows driver 1.100 (Pioneer_DDJ_SB_Driver_1.100.exe), signed by Pioneer DJ Corporation.
It gives the DDJ-SB its MIDI port and low-latency audio on Windows. The DDJ-SB DJ installer offers to run it, and Audio settings has an "Install the Pioneer DDJ-SB driver" button.
The driver is Pioneer DJ's software, not part of this project: check the licence shown by its own installer before redistributing it.
