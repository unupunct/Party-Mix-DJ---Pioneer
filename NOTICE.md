The MIDI layout in `public/ddjsb.js` (which note, control change and LED belongs to which control) is taken from the **Mixxx** project's Pioneer DDJ-SB mapping (`Pioneer-DDJ-SB.midi.xml` and `Pioneer-DDJ-SB-scripts.js`).

> This mapping for the Pioneer DDJ-SB was made by Joan Ardiaca Jove (joan.ardiaca@gmail.com) basing on the work of wingcom (https://github.com/wingcom/Mixxx-Pioneer-DDJ-SB), which in turn was based on the work of Hilton Rudham (https://github.com/hrudham/Mixxx-Pioneer-DDJ-SR). Published under the MIT license.

The MIT licence applies to that mapping. The behaviour behind each control in this program is new code.

## Pioneer DDJ-SB driver (Windows)
`drivers/Pioneer_DDJ_SB_Driver_1.100.exe` is the unmodified Windows driver installer published by Pioneer DJ Corporation (digital signature: CN=Pioneer DJ Corporation). It is Pioneer's software, covered by the licence its installer shows, and is included only so the DDJ-SB works on Windows without a separate download. If you prefer, delete the file and download the driver from Pioneer DJ's website.
